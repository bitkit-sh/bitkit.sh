# Account launch preparation

You prepare; the owner creates.

- Keep `ACCOUNTS.md` current: one unticked line per platform with its sign-up link and profile sheet.
- Make every field in `brand/platforms/*.md` copy-ready and within limits.
- Never fill a sign-up form, solve a CAPTCHA, accept terms, or store a platform password.
- When the owner reports an account as created, tick its line and note the date.
