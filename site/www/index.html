From a name to a running brand.

Four steps, all in your browser and your own Claude. No Cloudflare, GitHub, or devops knowledge needed.

Customize

Choose your name, handle, platforms, colors, type, and voice. bitkit checks every handle and bio against each platform’s rules.

Design with Claude

Add the bitkit plugin to your Claude. It fetches your kit and builds a full design system with you, then hands it back.

Deploy

bitkit publishes your website and your design system on Cloudflare: yours at your-name.bitkit.sh right away, or on your own domain.

Launch

Work through your checklist. You create each social account yourself, with every field ready to paste.

What you get.

One kit file drives everything, so changing a color or a handle updates every surface.

Design system

Tokens for light and dark, type, spacing, a brand book, and components, built in your Claude and published at kit.your-domain.

Website

An EmDash site on Cloudflare with an editor for your pages, seeded with copy in your brand’s voice.

Handles and profiles

One handle checked across 14 platforms, with a profile sheet for each: bio at the right length, image sizes, links.

Domain search

Live availability and pricing from Cloudflare Registrar. You register the one you pick.

Cloudflare services

Storage, database, AI, analytics, and email wired into your site, with nothing to configure.

Everything, downloadable

Export the whole kit anytime: brand files, website code, and a one-line setup command.

Bring your own Claude.

The bitkit plugin connects your Claude to your kit. Claude reads your kit, builds the design system with you, and sends it back for bitkit to publish.

Download the pluginSkill only

The plugin includes the bitkit skill and the connector at app.bitkit.sh/mcp. Claude never creates accounts or registers domains for you.
