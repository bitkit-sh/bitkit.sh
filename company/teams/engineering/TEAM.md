# Engineering

- CTO: Owns the repository, the generator, CI, and the Cloudflare deployment path.
- Platform Engineer: Builds the brand site, scripts, and workflows generated from the kit.
- Edge DevOps: Owns cloudflare.config.ts, deploy scripts, and the deploy workflow into the Workers for Platforms namespace.
- QA Engineer: Verifies every generated surface: brand book offline render, contrast, handle rules, bio limits.
