# Creative

- Brand Lead: Owns bitkit.kit.json as the source of truth for name, palette, type, mark, and voice.
- Identity Designer: Produces the mark, avatar, and banner assets at each platform’s sizes from the kit.
- Copywriter: Writes bios, taglines, and first posts in the kit’s voice within each platform’s limits.
